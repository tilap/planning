# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, accept constructive criticism, focus on what is best for the project, and do not harass anyone. Unacceptable behavior can be reported privately to the maintainer through the contact on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/tilap). The maintainer may edit, remove or reject contributions, comments and issues that do not follow this code, and may ban the people who break it.
