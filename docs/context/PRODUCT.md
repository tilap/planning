# Product

## Problem

A person follows several Google calendars (personal, shared, subscribed). They have to open Google Calendar one calendar at a time, which is slow on a phone, and nothing is readable without a network.

## Solution

An app installed on the phone's home screen shows one vertical list of all upcoming events. It opens straight on its local memory, even offline, and updates without blocking. It is read-only.

## User

Meant for one person, or a few. Only the Google accounts of the configured addresses (`ALLOWED_EMAILS`, any number) and, when the server has a database (`DATABASE_URL`), of the addresses the admins add in the app are allowed; each sees its own calendars. These addresses also have the **admin profile**, which gives access to the special admin features; the server decides it on every request from the current list. Every calendar this account can see is read.

## Behaviour

- **List**: "date, events, date, events…". Each timed event shows its start time, the end time under it, and its duration (45min, 1h30, 4h). An all-day event has no end time. Titles wrap on several lines. A day header spells the month in full ("mer. 18 novembre", "Wed, November 18"). On the right of each future day header, a light text gives the distance from today: "+3j" up to two weeks, then weeks and days ("+2s+1j", "+10s+2j"); "+3d" / "+2w+1d" in English. Nothing for today (which has its pill) nor for past days. The list opens on today; the past week is visible by scrolling up, and those days are outlined with a dashed border and their event titles are struck through.
- **Event cases**: one line per occurrence of a recurring event; "All day" at the top of the day for an event without a time; a multi-day event is repeated on every day it covers with "day 2/3".
- **Launch screen**: the logo, the name and a spinner (no text) over the whole page while the app loads, shown at least 0.8 seconds to avoid a flash, then the logo fades out over the opaque background, and the content fades in quickly once the launch screen is gone: the logo and the content are never visible together.
- **Details**: a tap opens a panel on the right, whatever the left-handed UI, with its cross on the left when the left-handed UI is on; the tapped row stays highlighted while it is open and fades back in 150ms when it closes (title, full date with the year (for a multi-day event, "From <first day>", "To <last day>" and "day 12/24" for the day that was opened), time range with its duration (45min, 1h30; not for an all-day or multi-day event), calendar, location, description (as text: the HTML Google may put in it is stripped, line breaks are kept, and the "Join with Google Meet" block of an invitation is hidden since the meeting has its own button; web addresses, email addresses and phone numbers are links, and so are the attendees' emails), who organizes it ("Organized by", with the email; not shown when the signed-in user organizes it, as in Google Calendar), attendees (the organizer always first; name, email and their answer: accepted, declined, maybe or awaiting reply; "you" marks the signed-in user), each with an icon; the location opens the chosen map app, by default Apple Plans on Apple devices and Google Maps elsewhere; video-meeting link, its dial-in number (a `tel:` link that also dials the PIN) with the PIN, and "Open in Google Calendar" when available). It closes with the cross, a swipe to the right, Escape, a tap on the dimmed page, or the back gesture. While a panel is open, the list behind it does not scroll. A panel slides in from its edge (the right for the details; for the settings the left, or the right in left-handed mode) and leaves the way it came, sliding back out to that edge; the swipe that closes it goes the same way, towards that edge: the panel follows the finger, leaves from where it was released when the swipe went past 80px, and comes back otherwise. Reduced motion: no slide.
- **Keyboard shortcuts**: single letters, for a keyboard: `p` opens or closes the settings, `s` opens the search, `r` syncs (online only), `n` and `b` scroll to the next and the previous day, `t` scrolls to today. They are ignored while typing in a field and with Ctrl, Cmd or Alt held. While the settings are open only `p` works, and none works while the details of an event are open.
- **Sync**: at launch, and when the app returns to the foreground after more than 15 minutes, with a discreet non-blocking message; in one tap on the sync button when online. No background sync. The synced window is, by default, the past 7 days and the next 3 months; the user changes it in the settings (past: 1 week, 2 weeks or 1 month; ahead: 1, 2 or 3 months, and no other value), and events that end before the start of the past range are deleted. A change of range syncs at once when online, and is applied at the next sync otherwise.
- **Failures**: the list stays displayed, a message gives the cause (network, quota, revoked access, Google outage, Calendar API disabled, calendar permission missing), and the date of the last successful sync stays visible. When the first sync fails and nothing was ever synced, a full screen explains the cause in plain words and offers "Retry" (or "Sign in again" when the cause needs a new sign-in) and "Sign out"; while that first sync runs, the screen says the calendars are loading.
- **Settings**: calendars visible or hidden (a new calendar appears visible), the "Personalization" section, in four blocks: language and formats (language (English, French, Spanish, Italian, German or Vietnamese, in that order in the selector; the device language by default, English when it is not one of them), hour format (12 h or 24 h), date format of the day headers (numeric `18/11/2026`, short `mer. 18 novembre` or long `mercredi 18 novembre`; short by default)), calendar data ("Calendar data": how far back, "-1 week", "-2 weeks" or "-1 month", and how far ahead, "+1 month", "+2 months" or "+3 months", of the events the app keeps; one week back and three months ahead by default; stored on the device), look (theme (paper, or the always-dark violet with a gradient background, rose and gold); each theme has a flat page color, seen when the page is pulled past its ends and given to the phone's status bar (violet: the first color of its gradient), appearance (light, dark, or the system choice; only offered with the paper theme, the others being always dark)), and map app for addresses (automatic, Plans or Google Maps), "Left-handed UI" and "Sounds". "Sounds" (off by default) plays a short discreet sound for each tap on a button or link, a click for each switch (higher when turned on, lower when turned off), a tick when a select changes, and a rising or falling sweep when a panel or sheet opens or closes; the cross that closes a panel stays silent, the closing sweep playing instead. The sounds are synthesized by the device, so they need no download and work offline; the phone's silent switch mutes them. The settings panel opens from the left (sliding to the right) and is closed by a swipe to the left, with its close button on the right; when "Left-handed UI" is on, everything is mirrored: it opens from the right, is closed by a swipe to the right and has its close button on the left (the text is not mirrored), and on a narrow screen the Settings and Sync buttons trade places. An **Administrator** section follows, for admins only and only when the server has a database for the allowed addresses (both known from the server while the settings are open, so offline it is hidden): its "Users" entry, with a chevron to the right, opens a sheet (the list of the addresses allowed to sign in: the admins, tagged, who cannot be removed, and the users added by an admin, each with a cross to remove it; a field and an "Add" button allow a new Google address, which can then sign in without the admin profile; removing one cuts its access at its next sync) that rises from the bottom over the whole screen, with rounded corners and a shadow, stopping 20px from the top; it closes with the cross (on the left when the left-handed UI is on), a tap on the dimmed page, a swipe down from its header, Escape or the back gesture, without closing the settings. The same sheet serves the other entries to come. Stored on the device only. A "Sign out" button revokes the Google access and empties the local memory while keeping the preferences.
- **Action bar**: on the list, Settings icon on the left, Search and Sync icons on the right. "Today" in the centre appears once the list has left today, and brings it back. On a narrow screen the bar is a pill floating at the bottom. From 768px wide it disappears: Settings and Sync sit in the top corners of the screen and "Today" in the bottom right corner, as an icon, with its text from 1024px. The date of the last sync sits at the top of the list and scrolls with it. The Search icon replaces the bar with a search bar of the same style (a text field, focused as soon as the bar opens, and a cross that puts the bar away and clears the filter): on a narrow screen the bar slides down out of the screen and the search bar slides up in its place; from 768px the corner icons stay and the search bar drops in at the top centre. The list is filtered on every change of the field. The filter keeps the events whose title, location, description or calendar name contain every word typed (ignoring case and accents); with none left, "No results" is shown. The bar is not shown on the sign-in screen, nor while the app is still choosing which screen to open.
- **Time zone**: the device's. All-day events keep their date whatever the time zone.
- **First launch**: a loading screen stays up until the app knows which screen to open, then a network is required (a "Sign in with Google" screen). With a local memory present and an expired session, the list stays readable and only the sync asks to sign in again. The app has no lock.

## Out of scope

Editing events, replying to invitations, notifications, background sync, a time zone setting, an app lock, reading through secret iCal addresses.
